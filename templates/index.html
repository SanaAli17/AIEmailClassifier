How it works

  • 1. Paste or upload Provide the email body as plain text or a .txt file.
  • 2. Pick a model Choose between three trained classifiers.
  • 3. Preprocessing HTML stripped, lowercased, tokenized, stopwords removed, WordNet-lemmatized.
  • 4. TF-IDF + ML The cleaned text is vectorised and scored.
  • 5. You get Label, category (work / personal / promotion for ham), confidence, sentiment, top-10 keywords, and live charts.
